QuantumScope — personal research storage

QuantumScope uses rclone to transfer and archive research files between the operator's computers and the operator's own Google Drive account. Its Google OAuth desktop client authorizes these file operations.

This is a private, single-operator research workflow, not a public sign-in service or a hosted application for third parties. This website describes the purpose of the OAuth client and does not itself request access to Google Drive.

See the privacy policy for details of Google data access and terms of use.
